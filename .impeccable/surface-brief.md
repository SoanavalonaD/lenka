# Surface Brief: Lenkā Madagascar E-Commerce

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- **Surface:** Boutique e-commerce complète (`/` Accueil, `/boutique` Catalogue, `/boutique/[slug]` Fiche Produit, `/cgv-livraison` CGV & Livraison, `/a-propos` À propos & Contact, Panier & Commande).
- **Mode:** Persuade (Accueil, À propos) & Operate (Catalogue, Fiche Produit, Panier/Checkout).

## Audience & Task
- Clients à Madagascar (Antananarivo et provinces) et diaspora/voyageurs souhaitant commander des sacs en raphia naturel tressé main, consulter les prix en Ariary (MGA), choisir leur taille et teinte, et régler par Mobile Money (MVola, Orange Money) ou en espèces à la livraison.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This surface operates as a sun-lit Malagasy raffia atelier archive and lookbook ("Carnet d'Atelier & Herbier de Fibres d'Antananarivo") rather than a generic SaaS/Shopify template of equal white cards. Every piece is presented with its weave density, hours of hand-braiding, natural dye origin, and direct Ariary ordering.

OWN-WORLD: Warm bleached-linen and unbleached raffia grounds (`#FAF7F2` and `#F3ECE1`), sun-baked laterite terracotta (`#C47256`), deep bark chocolate (`#382417`), and matte charcoal ink (`#1C1917`). Hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(56, 36, 23, 0.14)`) frame editorial split-screens, tactile swatch selectors, and atelier provenance stamps.

STORY: The visitor feels the warmth of Madagascar's natural raffia fibers ("Une lumière à porter"), explores flagship collections like "Zoe Linā", inspects the hand-woven texture and care rituals, and places an order in Ariary with immediate clarity on Antananarivo J+1 delivery and Mobile Money / Cash on Delivery options.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height asymmetric editorial split on warm linen (`#FAF7F2`): left column presents the serif headline "Une lumière à porter" with the sub-deck on Madagascar hand-crocheted raffia, primary CTA to explore the collection, and direct link to the "Zoe Linā" flagship line; right column features a tall architectural frame showcasing the sunlit raffia tote with an interactive weave-detail card and Ariary price specification.

FORM: Candidate 5 ("Carnet d'Atelier & Herbier de Fibres d'Antananarivo" — Architectural Atelier Lookbook & Provenance Index) on the 7-candidate list; seed:c375e8444c4fed51.
